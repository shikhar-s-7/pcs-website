01 The regulatory clock

Grid-forming is no longer optional in India.

As inverter-based renewables replace synchronous machines, India's grid is losing inertia and short-circuit strength. The system has already paid for it: in May 2026, a single 400 kV fault cascaded through the Khavda renewable zone and tripped 9,000 MW of generation in 16 seconds, while grid-following inverters injected reactive power into the fault.

The regulatory response is now a forced-demand clock. The Central Electricity Authority requires 100% grid-forming PCS on new BESS projects of 50 MW and above, and the draft Connectivity Regulations 2026 extend GFM requirements to renewable plants from July 2027, with EMT studies, demonstrated black start, and periodic re-verification.

Every serious grid-forming PCS operating in India today is imported. None is engineered or priced for Indian conditions.The supply gap Dhruvir is built to close
  • 01July 2026, CEA advisory. 100% grid-forming PCS mandated on all new BESS projects of 50 MW and above.
  • 02Draft Connectivity Regulations 2026. Minimum 15% grid-forming inverters on new renewable plants from July 2027; all BESS PCS to be grid-forming; EMT studies at design stage.
  • 03Commissioning evidence. Demonstrated GFM and black-start capability at commissioning, with periodic re-verification by load despatch centres.
  • 04Domestic supply gap. CEA has formally asked Indian manufacturers to declare GFM capacity. The mapping came back thin.

03 Engineered for Indian conditions

Imported hardware assumes a grid India doesn't have.

Global PCS platforms are designed around stiff grids, temperate climates, and utility buyers with deep commissioning budgets. Indian reality is different: 45-50°C ambients, dust ingress, weak-grid sites in the Rajasthan and Gujarat renewable zones, and erratic frequency.

Dhruvir's design baseline starts where imported datasheets end. Full power at 50°C, IP54-sealed enclosures with conformal-coated boards, and control stability proven at a short-circuit ratio of 1.0, not the SCR of 3+ that most GFM claims quietly assume.
